# 2026 Vape Content Brief

## Evidence-led themes for editorial planning

| Theme | Search-intent angle | Editorial boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Reusable and refillable devices | reusable vape, refillable pod system, replaceable coil | Explain jurisdiction-specific rules; do not call a device legal everywhere. |
| Battery safety and disposal | vape battery safety, how to recycle a vape | Direct readers to local hazardous-waste or approved battery collection routes. |
| Adult-only age controls | vape age verification, adult-only device access | Address business compliance and adult access only; never market to youth. |
| Product usability | USB-C vape charging, mesh coil, adjustable airflow, smart display | Describe function and care without medical, cessation, or performance guarantees. |
| Market access and labeling | ENDS compliance, vape packaging, product traceability | Recommend checking regional rules and official authorizations before sale. |

## Editorial standard

Every article should be original, adult-only in tone, concrete rather than generic, and avoid medical claims. Articles should state that legal requirements vary by country or region where a claim could otherwise be read as universal. The planned keyword set is a topic-prioritization list based on 2026 market and regulatory signals, not a claim of an exact Google Trends growth percentage.
